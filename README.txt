What this project appears to be:
  A churn-prediction / customer-retention style machine-learning project built
  around the UCI Online Retail dataset.

Why it matters in a portfolio:
  This is directly relevant to business cases where customer retention,
  subscription behavior, purchase falloff, and churn risk matter. It is a clean
  fit for companies such as Netflix, Amazon, or other businesses that care
  about customer loss, repeat behavior, and retention-oriented analytics.

Primary artifacts:
  The notebook is the editable analysis source.
  The PDF and DOCX act as readout/submission artifacts.
